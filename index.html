Camera & Mic Access

PrompterCam needs your camera and microphone. The mic drives the scroll; the camera records your delivery in the highest quality your device supports.

1
Tap Continue → below. Safari will show two permission prompts — one for Camera, one for Microphone. Both appear one after the other.
2
Tap Allow on both prompts. If you tap Don't Allow by mistake, follow step 3.
3
If you denied access, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera (and Microphone) and enable Safari. Then return here. Alternatively: Settings → scroll down to Safari → Camera / Microphone → set to Allow.
4
For the best experience, tap the Share ⬆ button in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. Removes the browser bar and locks the screen on during use.
5
If using a plug-in microphone, connect it before tapping Continue so it is detected automatically. iOS routes to the active audio input at permission time.

Your camera and microphone are used entirely on-device. Nothing is uploaded or stored remotely.
